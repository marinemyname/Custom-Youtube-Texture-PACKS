# zStarFlightz Texture Pack

Texture pack for **zStarFlightz**.

## Download

- In its pre-release
- `zStarFlightz-Texture-Pack.zip`

## Installation

1. Download the `.zip` file.
2. Do not unzip it.
3. Place it in your game’s texture pack folder.
4. Enable it from the in-game texture pack menu.
